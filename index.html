Why AI

Months of work, ready in days

A visual novel is a lot of work: a cast of characters with a face for every mood, a picture for every place, and hours of story. Made alone and by hand, a game like this takes months, sometimes years.

It makes the time possible

AI does the slowest part, the pictures, so your game is usually ready in 1–3 days instead of next year.

It makes the price possible

That much hand-drawn art would cost far more than most gifts. AI is what lets a one-off game, made for one person, start at $20.

Creative supervision

AI is fast, not careful. An experienced game developer reviews everything it produces, corrects the pictures by hand and writes the story. Expect good, consistent art, not the polish of a studio game that took years.

How it's made

Made with local AI, not the cloud

You should know exactly what you're buying, so here it is plainly.

AI-made art, directed by hand

The pictures are generated by AI models and corrected by hand. The story is planned and written by a person. Every game is played through before you get it.

It all runs locally

Your idea, your story and your art are made on our own computer, not on someone else's servers. Nothing is sent to ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney or any other AI company, and what you send is never used to train anything. The one exception is yours to choose: the optional voice-over and music add-on uses ElevenLabs, an online service.

Not a chatbot with a prompt

visualnovels.ai runs on its own story engine. AI models tailored for making visual novels run locally inside it, and it keeps characters and facts consistent, directs every picture and checks the results. That is why you can't get the same thing by typing into a general AI app.

Deleted when you ask

Ask, and your idea, script, art and game files are deleted from our computer. After that, the only copy is yours.

House rules

  • Stories about you and people you know are welcome. Share first names and describe each person in words (for example: brown skin, glasses, a little chubby).
  • No photos. Characters are made from written descriptions only.
  • Permission for real people. You confirm that everyone real in your story is happy to be in it. No celebrities or public figures.
  • Original characters. None from existing anime, games, films or books, though “inspired by” is fine.
  • Romance is between adults who look like adults. Flirty is fine; nothing explicit.
  • We'll say straight away if something can't be done, and may turn a request down.

FAQ

Questions

What is a visual novel?

A story you play. You read it on screen with pictures of the characters and places, and every so often you choose what to do. Your choices change what happens and how it ends. If you can read a text message, you can play one. Try the scene at the top of this page.

How is it made?

The art is generated with AI that runs locally and corrected by hand. The story is planned and written by a person. Every game is played through before it's delivered. The details are under Made with local AI, not the cloud.

Can it be about me and people I know?

Yes. Share first names and describe each person in words: hair, build, glasses, how they talk. Photos aren't accepted, and you'll be asked to confirm that everyone real in the story is happy to be in it.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes, and it makes a good one. Say who it's for and a dedication goes on the title screen. You get a download link you can pass on.

What do I need to play it?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC. Mac and phones aren't supported yet. The game is a single file of about 115 MB, delivered as a download link.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that safe?

Windows shows that for programs from small makers that don't carry a paid publisher certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 days after you've approved the plan.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. One round of changes to the story is included: lines, names, a scene that should go differently. Bigger reworks are quoted separately.

What if I change my mind?

You pay when you order. If you cancel before work has started, you get a full refund. If you've seen your story plan and don't want to go ahead, you get a partial refund: writing the plan is the biggest part of the work.

What happens to my idea?

It's used to make your game and nothing else, and it is never used to train anything. It stays on our own computer unless you add the voice-over and music option, in which case the lines of your story are sent to ElevenLabs to be voiced. Everything we hold, along with your game files, is deleted whenever you ask.

Does the game have sound?

Not as standard: the game is silent. For $20 more you can add full voice-over and music. Those are made with ElevenLabs, an online AI audio service, so the lines of your story are sent to them. The voices come from their library; no real person's voice is copied.
